Frequently asked questions

Will the entire resort be exclusive to Husna guests?

Yes. Only Husna guests and resort staff will be present for December 19–24, 2026.

Do we have to pay for food?

No. All-you-can-eat halal is included at every restaurant, pop-up, café, buffet and snack bar. Specialty items like lobster, tomahawk, private beach dinners and custom cakes are extra.

Is it always halal or only for these dates?

Only for our dates, December 19–24, 2026. After that the resort returns to normal operations.

Are flights included?

No. Flights and airport transfers are not included.

Are the rates per night or for the whole stay?

They cover the entire 5 nights. They are not per-night rates.

What airport should I fly into?

Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ). Recommended arrival on December 19 is between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM.

Is babysitting available?

Yes. $25 per hour for one child and $10 per hour for each additional child.

Is it suitable for senior guests?

Absolutely, as long as they are mobile. Husna welcomes seniors every year.

Is there a private pool for sisters?

There are two main pools; the second is dedicated to sisters. Neither is fully enclosed, so not private enough to remove hijab.

Can we tip in USD?

Yes. USD tips are preferred and guests are encouraged to tip generously.
