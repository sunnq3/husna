Frequently asked questions

Is this for families, or can singles and couples attend?

It’s for everyone! Every year we get a great blend of families, couples and groups of friends that come up and have a great time.

Are meals included?

Meals are not included, but all the restaurants at the resort will have halal options for this weekend only. Most guests split meals between resort restaurants and groceries they bring up.

Will the entire resort be exclusive to Husna guests?

Other guests may be present, but most of the resort will be occupied by Husna guests.

Is the resort always halal, or only for these dates?

Halal options are arranged for our dates only, January 29–31, 2027. After that the resort returns to normal operations.

Are there stores nearby?

Yes, there are ample stores within the vicinity of the resort — downtown Huntsville is about a 10 minute drive.

Is travel included in the rates?

No, flights and travel are not included in the rates.

Are the rates per person or per room?

Rates for Winter Classic Getaway are per room, not per person.

What airport should I fly into from out of town?

Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ).

What is the best time to arrive on Friday?

The best time to arrive is for jumuah on Friday, January 29.

Do we have to bring our own life jackets?

Yes, it’s better to for more accurate sizing and availability.

Should we bring our own table tennis rackets and sleds?

Yes, this is recommended.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Guests are expected to adhere to Husna’s attire guidelines at all times. For brothers: a minimum of a tank top and appropriately lengthened shorts; speedos are not permitted. For sisters: two-piece bikinis and non-Islamic one-piece swimsuits are not permitted, along with shorts, mini skirts, open tops, tank tops and open-back clothing; long dresses are acceptable. Husna reserves the right to ask any guest whose attire detracts from the experience of others to leave, at our discretion.

Is there private pool time for sisters?

For two hours following the Husna Night show on Saturday night, the pool will be reserved exclusively for sisters.

Is it a good idea to bring my senior parents?

As long as they are mobile, inshallah they will have a great time. We have plenty of seniors who grace us with their presence every year.
